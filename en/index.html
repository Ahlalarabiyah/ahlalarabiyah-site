The method

A progression designed to reassure, never to rush

Each notion builds on the last: letters, then vowels, prolongations, tanwīn, sukūn and shadda — always in color, always paired with tracing and reading exercises.

Progressive

One letter at a time, from isolated tracing to full words, without ever skipping a step.

No pressure

Every student moves at their own pace: no notion is imposed before they're ready.

Everything in color

Clear visual cues — harakat, tanwīn, prolongations — for effortless memorization.

Proven method

An approach already tested and validated with many students, in class and at home.

01

Isolated letters

Recognize and trace each letter of the Arabic alphabet.

02

Positions within a word

Find the letter at the start, middle, and end of a word.

03

Vowels & prolongations

Fatha, damma, kasra and moudoud to give sound to the letters.

04

Tanwīn, sukūn & shadda

The final nuances for reading any word accurately.

05

Independent reading

A dedicated reading page to consolidate everything learned.
