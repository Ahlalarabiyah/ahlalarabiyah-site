Privacy policy

A question about your data?

Questions: ahlou.sounnah.wal.arabiyah@gmail.com.

In short

At present, the site creates no accounts, has no forms, uses no cookies, no audience-measurement tools and no advertising. This page will be updated as soon as any of these is added.

What is stored on your device

To enable the educational activities and track your progress, the site uses your browser’s local storage (“localStorage”).

The information that may be stored includes in particular:

  • your best score for each module;
  • modules you chose to open despite a score below the success threshold;
  • modules or activity groups that have been unlocked;
  • an indicator showing whether you have already viewed certain game screens.

This information is used only to ensure the operation of the educational activities and to keep your progress on the device used.

It is not transmitted to a server by the local storage mechanism itself.

It remains stored in your device’s browser until you delete it or until it is erased when you clear the site’s data in your browser.

If you clear this data, your locally saved progress may be lost.

The local storage used for these features may fall under the consent exemption applicable to trackers strictly necessary to provide the service expressly requested by the user, subject to verification of their actual purposes and operation.

Third-party services

  • Hosting (GitHub Pages, USA): when you visit the site, the host technically receives your IP address and connection data to deliver and secure the service.
  • Google Fonts: fonts are loaded from Google servers, which sends your IP address to Google.
  • Contact by e-mail or WhatsApp: if you write to us, your details and message are processed (by Google for Gmail, by Meta for WhatsApp) to reply to you.
  • Payment: payments are not open yet. When they are, they will be handled by an online payment provider (Stripe), which receives the data needed for the payment; the publisher never keeps your card numbers.

Why and on what basis?

  • Running the site and its progress: delivering the requested service, local storage on your device.
  • Replying to your messages: legitimate interest and pre-contractual steps.
  • Payments and invoicing: performance of the contract and compliance with legal obligations.

Retention

  • Local storage: until you delete it.
  • Messages received: as long as needed to reply to you, then at most 3 years after the last exchange.
  • Invoicing data: the period required by the applicable law.

Transfers outside the European Union

Some providers (GitHub, Google, Meta) are located in the United States. These transfers rely on the safeguards offered by these providers (for example the “Data Privacy Framework” or the European Union’s standard contractual clauses).

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your data. To exercise them: ahlou.sounnah.wal.arabiyah@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

Children

The site is for all audiences, including children. The site creates no account and asks for no personal data: it can be used by children under their parents’ responsibility. If a child has written to us, a parent can ask for the messages to be deleted by contacting us.

Changes

Before accounts, referral, payments or the mobile app open, this policy will be completed (data collected, purposes, retention, providers, account deletion).
