Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most common questions about the method, free and paid content, and how to use the site.

Getting started

Who is Ahl Al Arabiyah for?

Everyone: children and adults, complete beginners and families alike. The method is progressive and practised without pressure, letter after letter.

Do I need to know Arabic already?

No. The method also suits people discovering Arabic for the first time: it starts with the letters, then adds step by step the vowels (harakāt), prolongations, tanwīn, sukūn and shadda.

What will I learn, and in what order?

The 28 letters of the Arabic alphabet, organized into 12 progressive review modules (with the pronunciation of each letter), always in colour and paired with tracing and reading exercises. Each notion builds on the last.

How much time should I spend each day?

It is better to work a little every day than a lot all at once: even ten to fifteen minutes, added up at different times of the day, can make a big difference. More tips are on the Tips page.

What equipment do I need?

At least: two pens (a regular one and a red one for corrections), two large-squared notebooks (lessons and exercises), headphones, a pencil and an eraser. Useful but optional: a ruler, small cards, a slate or whiteboard. Details in the Tips.

The activities

How do the dictations work?

Dictations come in 5 levels, from words to sentences. You listen to the audio, write in your notebook, then check with the correction. See the dictation tips.

How can I practise writing the letters?

In each module, the “Write the letters” button opens an exercise where you trace the letter with your finger, following the dotted line and the arrow, starting at the orange dot. The site then checks your tracing.

What is the training space?

A space to practise: dictations, concentration workshop, concentration challenges and reading.

Free and paid

What is free?

Modules 1 and 2 are free, as is the first group (the letter alif) of the training space.

Practical questions

Which devices can I use?

In a browser, on a computer, tablet or phone. To write the letters, a touch screen (finger or stylus) is ideal.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on the device you use: the site saves your progress in your browser, without an account. If you clear the site data or change device, it may be lost.

Which languages is the site available in?

French, English, Russian, German and Arabic. Use the language menu at the top of the page.

Does the site use cookies or advertising?

No: no cookies, no advertising and no audience-measurement tools.
