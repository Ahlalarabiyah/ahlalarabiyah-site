8. Referral program

Ahl Al Arabiyah offers a referral program allowing users to earn rewards when they recommend the platform to new users.

Each participating user has a personal referral code that they can share with friends and family.

Referrer rewards

Rewards are awarded according to the number of successful referrals:

  • 1 successful referral: one dictation unlocked;
  • 3 successful referrals: one free letters module;
  • 5 successful referrals: one free month of subscription;
  • 10 successful referrals: lifetime access to all Ahl Al Arabiyah dictations, including all dictations that will be added to the platform in the future.

This access is personal, unlimited in time and non-transferable. It gives access to existing dictations as well as to future dictations offered on the platform.

Rewards are awarded by tier and cannot be combined. Each tier entitles the referrer only to its own reward. Thus, when a user reaches 10 successful referrals, they receive only lifetime access to all dictations, without combining the rewards of the previous tiers.

Benefit for the referred person

Anyone using a valid referral code receives a 10% discount on their first eligible purchase from Ahl Al Arabiyah.

Conditions of validity

A referral is considered successful when the new user uses their referrer’s referral code on their first eligible order and the payment is actually validated.

Each new user can only be counted once. Cancelled, refunded or fraudulent orders do not entitle the referrer to a reward.

Referrals made using fake accounts, fictitious orders or any other fraudulent practice may be cancelled.

Rewards are personal and cannot be exchanged for their monetary value.
