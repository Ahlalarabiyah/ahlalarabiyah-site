About

An app designed by a teacher: Abdoullah ibnou Abi

This app was born from the real-world experience of an Arabic language teacher, Abdoullah ibnou Abi.

He first studied as a student at the Al-Ibanah institute in Egypt (2008), then became a teacher at the same institution (2011). He later opened his own institute and has taught independently since 2012. He is also the author of educational books.

With this experience, he knows well the difficulties students can face during their learning.

After several years of teaching diverse audiences, he created this app to offer a clear, progressive method adapted to the learner’s needs.

The goal is simple: to help every user learn with confidence, progress steadily, and develop their skills without stress or undue pressure.
